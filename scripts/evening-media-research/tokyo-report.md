# Tokyo venue media research

- Input rows: 59
- Verified photos: 8
- Needs illustration: 51
- Source-page domains: `openverse.org` (5), `commons.wikimedia.org` (3)
- Direct-asset domains: `upload.wikimedia.org` (7), `live.staticflickr.com` (1)
- Accepted licenses: CC0 1.0 (1), CC BY 2.0 (4), CC BY-SA 3.0 (1), CC BY-SA 4.0 (2)

## Verification method

Each venue was screened in source order: official media/press pages were usable only where explicit reuse terms existed, then exact-name Wikimedia Commons and Openverse searches were checked. Exact candidates were manually opened to confirm the same venue name, creator, and license; selected candidates were visually inspected where direct image retrieval was available. Google and Tripadvisor were treated as discovery-only and no user photo from either service was downloaded.

The result is intentionally adaptation-safe for editorial use: CC BY-NC and CC BY-ND/CC BY-NC-ND candidates were not accepted because ordinary editorial cropping or commercial publication could breach their restrictions. Neighborhood, landmark, and merely similar venue photos were excluded.

## Ambiguous or restricted cases

- `jp-b-starstar` and `jp-r-towers`: names are too generic to establish exact identity.
- `jp-a-red-tower`: the plausible Commons hit was Tokyo Tower illuminated red, not RED° TOKYO TOWER.
- `jp-a-street-kart`: reusable street-kart scenes did not establish the exact operator.
- `jp-a-yanaka-night`, `jp-a-zen`, `jp-a-samurai`, and `jp-a-night-kamakura`: generic experience labels did not resolve to unique operators or venues.
- `jp-r-tayori`: exact images were CC BY-NC only.
- `jp-a-noh` and `jp-a-kingyo`: exact images were CC BY-NC-ND only.
- `jp-b-albatross`: exact images were CC BY-ND only.
- The verified HAGI CAFE, TsuruTonTan Roppongi, and Fuunji assets show venue-specific menu/food rather than building exteriors; source titles explicitly identify the venue.
